How to play 7Cards

We play with a standard 52-card deck. All cards are shuffled and shared as evenly as possible between 2–8 players. No jokers.

  1. The player holding the 7 of hearts (♥) must play it first.
  2. Next, you can add the 6♥ or 8♥, open a new suit with any other 7, or extend an opened suit one rank at a time.
  3. For each suit, cards build downward to Ace (1) and upward to King (13).
  4. On your turn, play one highlighted legal card. Pass only when you have no legal card.
  5. A round ends when someone plays their last card. Everybody else gets penalty points for cards remaining: A=1, J=11, Q=12, K=13.
  6. After your chosen 5–100 rounds, the player with the lowest total penalty wins. Ties share the win.

Online games work through peer-to-peer connections. The host must keep their tab open. There is no account, payment, betting, or money involved.
