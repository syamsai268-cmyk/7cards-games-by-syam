How to play 7Cards

We play using 44 cards from a standard deck, removing every Ace and every 2. The remaining cards (3 through King in all four suits) are shuffled and dealt as evenly as possible to 2–8 players. No jokers.

  1. The player holding the 7 of hearts (♥) must play it first.
  2. Next, you can add the 6♥ or 8♥, open a new suit with any other 7, or extend an opened suit one rank at a time.
  3. For each suit, cards build downward to 3 and upward to King (13). No Ace or 2 can be played.
  4. On your turn, play one highlighted legal card. Pass only when you have no legal card.
  5. A round ends when someone plays their last card. Everybody else gets penalty points for cards remaining: 3=3, J=11, Q=12, K=13.
  6. Play as many rounds as you like. After each completed round, scores are added together. When the host chooses End match, the player with the lowest total penalty wins. Ties share the win. An unfinished round does not count.

Online games use a room relay for card play and optional live voice chat. The host must keep their tab open. There is no account, payment, betting, or money involved.
